## Table 3 — Information Sharing & Disclosure (who we share with, and why)

| Recipient | How & why we share |
|---|---|
| **Panameer Users** | As part of the Service, Panameer provides a platform that facilitates interactions between users. In order to provide the Services, Panameer shares your information with other users. For example, Panameer may share the jobs you post and your profile data publicly on our platform, return your information in search results, share your information with other Panameer users who might be interested in the content you shared, or share your information with other users as part of a potential or active **Work Order**. |
| **Service Providers** | We may employ service providers and individuals to facilitate our Service, to provide the Service on our behalf, to perform Service-related services (e.g., maintenance, database management, web analytics, online advertising, payment processing, fraud detection and improvement of Panameer's features) or to assist us in analyzing how our Service is used. These service providers may include analytics partners, advertising partners, payment processors, identity verification companies, security companies, generative AI partners, or other merchants. These third parties may have access to your Personal Information in order to perform these tasks on our behalf. The Service includes a two-sided marketplace, which results in platform information pertaining to both parties on either side of an interaction. For example, a Service Buyer's review is inherently about a Provider, a Service Buyer job post is work history for a Provider, and Service Buyer spend is part of earning history for a Provider. Thus, if a Service Buyer or Provider chooses to use a feature that involves information being shared with a service provider, the platform information associated with that user's account — including information described above — may be shared with that service provider. |
| **Generative AI Partners** | We provide certain features of our Service by integrating trusted generative AI service providers. We may share the information you provide while using these features with these generative AI service providers, as well as additional information necessary to utilize the feature.
